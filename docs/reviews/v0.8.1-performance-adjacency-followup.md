# V0.8.2 Performance And Adjacency Follow-up

Date: 2026-09-17
Scope: V0.8.2 changes after the V0.8.1 checkpoint plus read-only `embodied-nav` validation
Checkpoint baseline: `804f3594a76825bd285dd57bdc53cd6055033d4f`

## Outcome

- Full `rf --project embodied-nav status` fell from roughly 2-3 minutes to 39.351-58.796 seconds across two post-change runs on the same local machine.
- Operation-local reuse removes repeated Knowledge inventory, Corpus source/matrix verification, extraction status verification, and per-edge PaperAdjacency ledger/currentness work.
- The human-reviewed 24-case adjacency benchmark improved from TP=10, FP=0, FN=4, TN=10, F1=0.8333 to TP=12, FP=0, FN=2, TN=10, F1=0.9231.
- Semantic generator `semantic-v2.2` adds two evidence-bounded cases: an exact `compared_with` baseline may receive a reported failure link, and a broader-family failure without matched context becomes a low-score `boundary_case` only when the target extraction reports no failure condition.
- Existing accepted semantic edges remain current by verifying against the generator version recorded on each edge; a newer generator does not silently invalidate a prior human acceptance when its source evidence is unchanged.

## Performance measurements

Before the change, isolated component timings were approximately:

- Knowledge summary plus check: 55.624 seconds.
- EvidenceGraph summary: 82.880 seconds.
- Corpus summary: 17.990 seconds.
- PaperAdjacency summary: 6.544 seconds.

After the change:

- Knowledge inventory used by both summary and check: 12.186 seconds.
- EvidenceGraph summary: 17.886 seconds.
- Corpus summary: 4.057 seconds.
- PaperAdjacency summary: 3.657 seconds.

Timings are local wall-clock observations with visible run-to-run variation, not a portable performance guarantee.

## Remaining false negatives

The evaluator now emits an explicit diagnosis instead of treating every false negative as a generator defect:

- `PAPER-0028 extends_method PAPER-0004`: the accepted extraction contains `compared_with`, but no explicit `extends` relation. Comparison alone does not prove extension.
- `PAPER-0029 same_problem PAPER-0030`: the papers share an accepted Method family, but their accepted Problem/Task keys are not aligned. Method-family overlap alone does not prove the same problem.

Closing either miss requires source review and a human-reviewed extraction or concept decision in the research project. ResearchOS must not hard-code these paper IDs or infer the relation from insufficient evidence.

## Verification and boundaries

- All 118 collected TEST/MOCK tests pass after the changes; `compileall`, CLI loading, and `git diff --check` also pass.
- The real benchmark was evaluated read-only; no candidate build, review, EvidenceGraph write, or project migration was performed.
- The new preview contains 47 candidates, including 3 `boundary_case` candidates; 35 candidates are outside the 24-case benchmark and therefore remain unmeasured rather than presumed correct.
- These metrics validate behavior only on the named reviewed benchmark. They do not establish literature truth, novelty, or a scientific result.
